# A: Audit

Rank assumptions in the component map by how much the outcome depends on them. For each, record:

- Claim and status: supported fact, convention, or unknown.
- Evidence and limitations, or evidence needed to resolve the unknown.
- Consequences of removing or inverting the assumption.

Assess whether conventions are necessary rather than rejecting them automatically. Distinguish them from explicit user constraints. Identify the facts and constraints available for recombination and the consequential unknowns.
