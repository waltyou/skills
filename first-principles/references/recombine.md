# R: Recombine

Build three candidates that differ in mechanism or organization. For each, identify supporting facts and constraints, the convention it relaxes, and its main failure point. Label new premises as assumptions to test.

If evidence supports fewer candidates, explain why. Keep feasibility conditional where essential facts remain unknown. Compare against the user's success criteria; novelty alone is not evidence of usefulness.

In staged mode, invite selection or revision before Experiment. If the user simply asks to continue, test all remaining candidates.
